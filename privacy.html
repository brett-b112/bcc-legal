Privacy Policy

The BrettCoversCapital publisher (“the app”) posts BrettCoversCapital’s own videos to its own social media accounts.

What the app receives

When the account owner authorizes the app on TikTok, TikTok gives it an access token and basic profile information (an account ID, display name and username), which the app uses only to post videos and link to them.

How it is stored

Tokens are kept in a file readable only by the owner on the owner’s own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to anyone other than TikTok, and they are used for no other purpose.

Other people’s data

The app does not collect data from viewers or any other TikTok users.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services; the saved token is then deleted.
